- Six-class export: `vision/dataset_v2/BeltGuard-Damage-Detection.v1i.yolov11`, version 1, workspace `om-jfwls`, [export source](https://universe.roboflow.com/om-jfwls/beltguard-damage-detection-dkb2n/dataset/1). The repository-root export is a duplicate of this dataset.

Keep upstream notices and dataset attribution when sharing. The team must confirm ownership and permission for captured images and model distribution before public release.
